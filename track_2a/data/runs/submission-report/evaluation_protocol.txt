RECONSTRUCTED EVALUATION PROTOCOL — NOT THE ORIGINAL SAVED PROMPT

Purpose and provenance
This document reconstructs the interactive instructions used for qualitative
assessment of complete interview-coach outputs. It was written after that
assessment; it is not a verbatim record of one original prompt and was not
used to regenerate the existing findings.

The assessment model was GPT-6.1-Sol in the coding-assistant workflow, as
identified by the project author. The saved label files do not independently
record the model/version or a complete system-and-user prompt. The local
label-writing scripts store assistant-authored observations and validate
quotes; they do not invoke a separately configured GPT judge API.

Relevant saved material (paths relative to track_2a/)
- data/interview/feedback_guidelines.json: organiser feedback rules.
- data/interview/criteria.json: candidate-assessment rubric, not a numerical
  scorecard for coach quality.
- data/manual_review/versions/interviews-v17/sample.json: evaluated outputs.
- data/manual_review/versions/interviews-v17/labels.json: saved findings.
- src/experiments/label_uniform_review.py and label_optional_review.py:
  saved rule interpretations and case-specific observations.
- data/runs/full-interviews-v17-optional-recap/evaluation/comparison.json:
  programmatically calculated runtime/availability metrics.
These generated/experimental resources remain local, outside the submission
runtime and Git history. Their availability is not a promise of deterministic
reproduction of model outputs.

Reconstructed task instructions
Evaluate the coach's behaviour over each complete interview, including the
final recap. Use the organiser's constructive-feedback guidelines. Read the
actual question, candidate answer, coach response and relevant preceding
context before judging a passage. Use the complete interview as the evaluation
unit: a greeting alone does not require all final-feedback behaviours.

Check:
1. Respectful, age-appropriate tone and understandable, concise language.
2. Attention to the actual answer and coherent transitions between topics.
3. Concrete feedback grounded in the candidate's visible statements.
4. Unsupported praise, invented abilities/experiences or employer facts.
5. Whether a follow-up addresses a real gap rather than a sufficient answer.
6. Useful, proportionate next steps and explicit control over optional practice.
7. Whether explanations and evidence actually support one another; a literal
   quotation alone does not establish a correct interpretation.

Distinguish these sources:
- Candidate statements are evidence about what the candidate said, not proof
  of independently observed competence.
- Hypothetical scenario answers describe proposed actions, not past experience.
- Only the supplied posting establishes company facts.
- Coach explanations and ratings are outputs to assess, not ground truth.
- Older comparison recaps and context-only material are not current coach
  output and must not be cited as current evidence.

For each applicable guideline, state whether it is met, violated or uncertain.
Where it cannot be assessed, explain why rather than treating absence as a
failure. Cite an exact coach-output passage for a claimed violation, and
include the relevant question/candidate context in the reasoning. Do not infer
voice, gestures or body language from text. Do not reward unnecessary advice
or penalise an adequate small-talk answer for lacking a professional example.

Summarise the main strengths and recurring weaknesses in plain language.
Do not turn qualitative findings or candidate scores into an invented overall
coach performance score. Keep GPT's interpretation separate from deterministic
metrics such as request counts, completion and presence of practice choices.

Scope of the report's numbers
The 1,178-call workload combines recorded conversation and recap traces for
416 candidate answers: 2.83 requests per answer, maximum session average 3.50.
Some conversation outputs were retained while recaps were regenerated. These
figures are not a newly generated end-to-end run of every subsequent code fix.
Candidate simulation and supplementary translation/probe calls are outside
this coach-workload aggregate. A returned recap can still have missing
assessments. No calibrated quality score or repeated-run consistency metric
was established by this procedure.
